Outgoing webhooks of slot

OpenVP sends a JSON POST to your URL when a signal changes, or when a condition becomes true (the same language as watches, for example Room_On=1 && Proj_Power=0 for 2s). Failed requests are tried again after 1 s, 5 s and 30 s. With a signing secret each request carries X-OpenVP-Signature: sha256=…, the HMAC-SHA256 of timestamp + "." + body, and X-OpenVP-Timestamp. The URL, headers and secret are saved with the slot, so they are in its snapshots.

Incoming webhooks

Anyone who has a hook's address can set signals in its slot with POST /hooks/<token> and a body like {"signal":"Room_On","value":1} or {"pulse":"Button1"}. The token is the only credential, so treat the address like a password: it is for one slot and (if you list signals) only those signals, can be switched off or revoked, and is rate limited. These addresses work without the access key, but not through the internet tunnel.

OSC

Open Sound Control over UDP. Receive: messages to /openvp/slotNN/<signal> with one value set the signals ticked Receive below (digital: int or float, or no value to pulse, or /pulse on the end; analog: an int 0-65535, or a float 0 to 1 as a fraction of full scale; serial: a string). Send: each change of a signal ticked Send goes to the target as one message (digital and analog as an int, serial as a string).

Art-Net

Analog signals become DMX channels: 0-65535 is scaled to 0-255, or in 16-bit mode the high byte goes on the channel and the low byte on the next one. ArtDMX packets go to the target at a steady rate whether or not anything changed. A node's own address, or a broadcast address such as 2.255.255.255 or 255.255.255.255. Optionally OpenVP also listens on UDP 6454 and sets signals ticked Receive from incoming ArtDMX.

Prometheus metrics

OpenVP serves its metrics in the Prometheus text format at /metrics: process CPU, memory and threads; per slot the state, events per second, busy percent, queue depth, devices online, panel connections, crash and restart counts and alerts; and the integrations' counters. It needs the access key, except from this computer; set OpenVP:MetricsPublic=true to drop that. It is never served on the internet tunnel's port.
