Scheduled actions

Do something at a time: restart a slot every night, start or stop a program, load a program version or a file, run a backup, or set or pulse a signal. Times are the processor's local time (), and change with daylight saving. A time that doesn't exist on the day the clocks go forward runs just after the gap; a time that happens twice runs the first time.
