    The demo is running. A test panel for slot 1 (IP ID 03) opened in a new window.
    
    
      
      
    1. On that panel, press and hold the button labelled 10 (digital join 10).
      2. 
      
    2. Slot 1's SIMPL# program passes it over the EISC to slot 2's SIMPL Windows program, which lights digital 10 on its own panel. Open slot 2's panel to see its button 10 turn green while you hold the one on slot 1.
      3. 
      
    3. It works the other way too: press 10 on slot 2's panel and slot 1's button 10 lights up.

  Support bundle for slot 
  
    A zip with the slot's log, its recent trace (if a debugger was open), compatibility report, program summary, metrics, settings, activity and the OpenVP version and OS. It never contains the access key, and leaves out the program, its NVRAM and User files unless you tick the box.
    
  
  
    

    

    
  
    Welcome to OpenVP
    
  
    OpenVP runs Crestron SIMPL# Pro (.cpz, .dll) and SIMPL Windows (.lpz, .smw) programs on any computer. There is no license server. You get up to 10 slots, browser test panels and a debugger. It is not affiliated with Crestron.
    
  
    What to do first
    
  
      
    
    1. Pick a slot in Program slots.
      2. 
    
    2. Choose your program file and click Load & start. In the desktop app the .sig comes along, and the slot can reload when you recompile.
      3. 
    
    3. In the IP table, click a device and open a test panel.
      4. 
    
    4. Click Open debugger to watch signals change.
      5. 
  
    
  
    No program yet? Try the demo loads two samples that talk to each other, and Templates in the top bar has starter projects to download.
    
  
    Good to know
    
  
      
    
    • The Status board shows every slot at a glance.
      • 
    
    • History takes snapshots of a slot and rolls back to them. Recordings record joins and replay them.
      • 
    
    • The Help link on every page explains each button. Hold Shift and click a button, or hover it and click the ?, to open its help.
      • 
    
    • OpenVP tells you whether a program will run as soon as you choose it, and Activity lists who did what.
