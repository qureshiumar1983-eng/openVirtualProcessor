# Code of conduct

OpenVP follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't accepted in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments or block people who break these rules.
